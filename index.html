About Me

Turning Complex Tech into Simple Solutions

With over 10 years of experience in IT and digital transformation, I specialise in making technology work for people, not the other way around. My background spans DevOps engineering, business systems analysis, Power Platform development, and comprehensive IT infrastructure management. I'm particularly passionate about helping organisations that are often underserved by traditional tech consultancies.

I hold a BSc (Honours) in Digital and Technology Solutions, specialising in Software Engineering from The Open University.
